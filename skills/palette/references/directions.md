# Curated colour directions

51 ground + figure pairs, each chosen by a person to avoid the generic palettes AI-built sites reach for. Use them
as starting points and as proof of what a specific direction looks like, not as a menu every brief must pick
from. A direction you build yourself, from the brief and the SKILL.md process, is just as valid.

Each one lists ground (background and large surfaces), figure (headings, type, logo) and an optional accent, in
HEX and OKLCH. Contrast was checked when the direction was curated; check it again for every text pair you
actually use. Grouped by structure (how the two colours relate) and foundation (light or dark ground).
