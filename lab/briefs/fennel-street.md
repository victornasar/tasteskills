# Brief: Fennel Street Bakery

**Register:** warm, local, handmade. The opposite of [tallyroll.md](tallyroll.md).

**What it is:** a neighbourhood bakery run by two sisters, Ana and Rita Duarte. Sourdough, Portuguese pastries
and a few seasonal cakes. Most customers walk in; some order cakes ahead.

**Audience:** people who live within a few streets, and people who heard about the custard tarts. Mostly on
their phones, often checking whether the shop is open.

**The page must:** show what's baked, when the shop is open and where it is, and make cake orders easy.
