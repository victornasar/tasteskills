Hello there

I'm Mira, and I draw for a living.

I grew up between Lagos and Lisbon, filling sketchbooks on long bus rides. Today I work from a sunny studio above a bakery, mixing gouache, risograph textures and digital collage into pictures that feel handmade and alive.

I love projects that tell a story — whether that's a 32-page picture book, a magazine cover on a Tuesday deadline, or a tin of tea that deserves to be kept long after it's empty.
