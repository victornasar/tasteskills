Our Process

From seed to your cup

Great coffee is a chain of careful hands. Here's our part of it.

01

Source with intention

We visit our partner farms each harvest and pay an average of 2.4× the Fair Trade minimum, published openly on every bag.

02

Roast in small batches

Each coffee gets its own profile, tuned through dozens of sample roasts to highlight what makes it unique.

03

Ship at peak freshness

Orders are roasted Monday or Thursday and packed in compostable, valved bags within hours of cooling.
