Listen

ii.

Frame

Turn fuzzy goals into a crisp problem statement and measurable outcomes the whole team signs.

Prototype

03 — About

I believe the best interfaces disappear — leaving people with the feeling that they did it themselves.

I started out as an architect, which taught me that structure is a form of kindness. Since then I've led design at two startups, helped a 150-year-old bank go mobile-first, and built design systems that scale across hundreds of contributors.

Today I work independently with small, ambitious teams. Away from the screen I'm usually in a ceramics studio, or wandering a city with a film camera.
