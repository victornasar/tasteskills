About the writer

I write for people who'd rather stay a month than see ten cities in a week.

I started Far & Slow in a hostel in Valparaíso after my third notebook ran out of pages. What began as letters home turned into a place for long-form essays, honest guides, and the small moments that never make it into a magazine edit.

My work has appeared in print and online travel publications, and I occasionally lead small writing workshops for travellers who want to bring better stories home. When I'm not moving, I'm in a drafty flat in Lisbon, arguing with my sourdough.

— Mara
