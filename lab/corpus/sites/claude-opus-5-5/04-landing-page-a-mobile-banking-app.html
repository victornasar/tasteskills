FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Is Tidewell a real bank?

Tidewell is a financial technology company. Banking services are provided by our FDIC-member partner bank, so your deposits are insured up to $250,000.

Are there really no monthly fees?

Yes. The Essential plan has no monthly fees, no minimum balance, and no overdraft fees. Paid plans are optional and can be canceled anytime.

How long does it take to open an account?

Most people are approved in under 5 minutes. You'll need a government-issued ID and to be at least 18 years old.

Can I deposit cash?

Yes — deposit cash at over 90,000 participating retail locations, or move money in from any external bank account.

What if I lose my card?

Freeze it instantly in the app, then order a replacement with one tap. Your virtual card keeps working in the meantime.
