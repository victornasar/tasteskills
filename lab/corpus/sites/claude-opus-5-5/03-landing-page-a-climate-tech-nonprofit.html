What we do

Three ways we turn climate risk into community wealth

We pair flexible capital with technical support so that residents, not distant investors, decide what gets built and who benefits.

Community Solar & Storage

Grants and zero-interest bridge loans for cooperatives, schools, and tribal nations building shared solar with battery backup.

  • Feasibility studies at no cost
  • Co-op formation & legal support
  • Bill savings shared with members
Explore solar projects →

Coastal & Heat Resilience

Nature-based defenses like living shorelines, urban tree canopy, and cooling centers designed with the people who live there.

  • Wetland & dune restoration
  • Resilience hubs with microgrids
  • Neighborhood heat-mapping
Explore resilience projects →

Green Workforce Pathways

Paid apprenticeships in solar installation, weatherization, and grid maintenance, with job placement at partner employers.

  • Stipends & childcare support
  • Industry-recognized certifications
  • Union and employer partnerships

Our model

From kitchen-table idea to power on the grid

Every project follows the same principle: those closest to the problem lead the solution.

Listen

Local organizers host listening sessions to surface what residents actually need.

Design

Our engineers and community members co-design a project, budget, and ownership plan.

Fund

We blend donor grants with low-cost capital to cover what banks won't.

Own

The community owns the asset, and the savings and revenue stay local for decades.
