The Philosophy
“The best photographs aren't posed. They're the quiet glance across the aisle, the laugh you didn't know anyone saw, the hand that found yours without looking.”
Hello, I'm Elara

Part storyteller, part quiet observer

For over a decade I've had the privilege of standing just beside some of the most meaningful days of people's lives. I photograph with a documentary heart and an editorial eye — guiding gently when it matters, and disappearing when it doesn't.

I shoot on both digital and medium-format film, and I travel wherever love takes me: misty coastlines, city rooftops, vineyards, and backyard ceremonies alike.
