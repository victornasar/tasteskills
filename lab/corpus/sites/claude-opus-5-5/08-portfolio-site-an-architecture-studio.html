The studio

We believe the best buildings are patient. They are made from few materials, built to be repaired, and tuned to the particular light of their place.

Strata Office is an architecture and interiors practice of thirty-one people working from studios in London and Lisbon. We take on a small number of projects each year so that the partners remain closely involved from first sketch to final detail.

Our work spans private houses, affordable housing, libraries, civic buildings and workplaces. Across every scale we look for the same things: a clear idea, honest construction, and spaces that feel generous to the people who use them.

We are signatories to Architects Declare and measure the whole-life carbon of every project we deliver.

How we work

  1. Listen

    Every project begins with long conversations — with clients, neighbours and the people who will live or work in the building.

  2. Read the site

    We map sun, wind, views, history and ground. The site sets the brief as much as any schedule of accommodation.

  3. Choose material

    A restrained palette, sourced close to home where possible: timber, brick, lime, stone. Materials that age with grace.

  4. Draw the detail

    We draw every junction by hand and stay on site through construction, working alongside makers until handover.
