About the Photographer

I believe the in-between moments tell the truest story.

For over a decade I have photographed weddings across coastal towns, vineyards, and city rooftops. My approach is quiet and unobtrusive. I want you to forget the camera is there, so that the laughter, the tears, and the stolen glances are captured exactly as they happened.

Every gallery is hand-edited with a soft, film-inspired palette, and delivered with care so your memories feel as warm in twenty years as they do the morning after.

— Amelia
