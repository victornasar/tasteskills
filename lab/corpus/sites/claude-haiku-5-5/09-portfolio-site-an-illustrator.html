Hello, I'm Ava.

I'm an illustrator based in Brooklyn, working with editors, publishers, and small brands. I grew up with a sketchbook in every bag and I still believe a good drawing can carry more feeling than a paragraph of copy.

My process is slow and tactile. I sketch in pencil, paint in gouache and ink, and bring in paper textures and scans so the work keeps the trace of a human hand.
