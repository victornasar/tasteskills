Home / Our Story
Since 2014

Roasting with patience and care.

From a garage to a roastery.

Ember & Bean started with a secondhand drum roaster, a kitchen scale, and a stubborn belief that coffee tastes better when someone actually cares how it's roasted.

Today we still roast in small batches, still taste every lot before it leaves the building, and still write our roast profiles by hand. We work with a handful of farms we've visited, paying prices that make the work worth doing.

Thank you for being part of it.
