FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does setup take?

Most teams are sending invoices within an hour. Connecting your accounting system and importing customers is typically the longest step, and it takes only a few minutes.

Which accounting systems do you integrate with?

We offer two-way sync with QuickBooks Online, Xero, and Sage Intacct. Enterprise plans include NetSuite and custom ERP connections through our API.

Can my customers pay without creating an account?

Yes. Every invoice includes a secure payment link. Customers can pay by ACH, card, or wire without signing up for anything.

How do you handle payment processing fees?

ACH payments are charged a flat fee per transaction. Card payments carry standard processing rates, which can be passed to the customer or absorbed by your business. Full pricing details are shown at checkout setup.

Is my financial data secure?

We are SOC 2 Type II compliant, encrypt all data in transit and at rest, and support SSO and role-based permissions on Enterprise plans.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no long-term contracts. You can cancel from your account settings, and you will keep access through the end of your billing period.
