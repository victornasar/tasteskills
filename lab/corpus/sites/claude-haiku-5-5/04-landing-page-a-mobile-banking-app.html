FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Vault a bank?

Vault offers banking services through our licensed banking partner. Deposits are FDIC-insured up to the applicable limit.

How long does it take to open an account?

Most people finish signup in about ten minutes. Identity verification is usually completed within a few minutes after you submit your documents.

Are there any monthly fees?

The Essential plan is free forever. Plus and Business have a monthly subscription that you can cancel at any time.

Can I use Vault without a physical card?

Yes. You can start with a virtual card instantly and order a physical card later at no extra cost.

How do I contact support?

Reach our team 24/7 through in-app chat, email, or phone. Plus and Business members get priority response times.
