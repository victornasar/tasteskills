Our Mission

Bridging the gap between innovation and impact.

Brilliant climate ideas often stall before they reach the people who need them most. We close that gap by funding early-stage research, providing hands-on technical support, and connecting promising solutions with public and private partners.

Stories

Solutions in action

Meet the people and communities turning climate ideas into lasting change.

Clean Energy

A solar microgrid keeps a village's clinic running

After a storm knocked out power for a week, residents partnered with our accelerator to build a battery-backed microgrid that now powers a clinic and 40 homes.

— Maria Okonkwo, community lead

Urban Resilience

Cooling corridors for a hotter city

Our grant funded tree-lined corridors and reflective pavement in a neighborhood where summer temperatures were 8°C above the city average.

— Devon Park, city planner

Open Data

Public data that changed a county's plan

Local officials used our open emissions dataset to redesign their transit plan, cutting projected transport emissions by nearly a fifth.

— Priya Raman, policy analyst
