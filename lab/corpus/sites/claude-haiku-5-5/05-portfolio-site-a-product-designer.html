About me

I'm a product designer who believes the best software feels almost invisible. I care about the quiet details: a clear empty state, a label that doesn't need explaining, a flow that respects someone's time.

My process blends research, rapid prototyping, and close collaboration with engineers. I'm most energized when I'm working with small, focused teams on problems that matter.

Before design, I spent three years in customer support, which taught me to listen first and to notice where people get stuck.
