FAQ

Questions, answered

Does Quill AI train on my notes?

No. Your notes are used only to provide features to you. We never use your content to train models shared with other users.

Can I import from other apps?

Yes. You can import Markdown, plain text, PDF, and common exports from Notion, Evernote, and Apple Notes.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Notes are saved locally and sync automatically the next time you're online. AI features require a connection.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel from your settings page at any time. You'll keep Pro access until the end of your billing period.
