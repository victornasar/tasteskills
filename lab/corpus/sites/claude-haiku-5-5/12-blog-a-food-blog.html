About Savory & Sweet

Written by Maya Lindqvist

Savory & Sweet started in a tiny apartment kitchen with one oven that ran hot and a stack of handwritten recipe cards. Today it's where I share everything I cook, bake, and taste on the road.

My philosophy is simple: good food doesn't need to be complicated. I focus on seasonal ingredients, honest techniques, and recipes you'll actually make on a Tuesday night.

Thanks for being here. Pull up a chair, and let's get cooking.
