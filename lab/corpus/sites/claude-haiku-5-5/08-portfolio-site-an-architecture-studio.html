About the studio

Quiet architecture for demanding places.

Studio Form is a practice of fifteen architects, designers and makers working between two cities. We design houses, libraries, workshops and public spaces with a preference for restraint.

Our work begins with the site: its light, its materials, its history and the way people already move through it. From there we make few gestures, but make each one carefully, prioritising proportion, daylight and durable materials over novelty.

We collaborate closely with clients, engineers and craftspeople from the first sketch to the final detail, and we remain involved long after handover to see how spaces age.

Our approach

Three principles guide every project.

i.

Read the place

Before drawing a line we study the site's climate, context and history, and listen to how the people who use it live and work.

ii.

Make less, mean more

We favour a small number of precise moves. Proportion, daylight and the sequence of spaces do most of the work.

iii.

Build to last

We specify honest, repairable materials and detail buildings to age gracefully, reducing energy use across their lifetime.
