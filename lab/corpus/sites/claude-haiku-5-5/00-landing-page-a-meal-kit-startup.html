Questions

Frequently asked

Can I skip a week or cancel anytime?

Yes. You can skip, pause, or cancel from your account dashboard up until the Tuesday before your scheduled delivery. No fees, no hassle.

Do I need cooking experience?

Not at all. Every recipe card includes step-by-step photos, and most meals require only basic kitchen tools and one pan.

How fresh is the food?

We source produce from local farms and partner suppliers, then pack orders within 24 hours of shipping. Ingredients arrive chilled in insulated, recyclable packaging.

Can I accommodate allergies or dietary needs?

Yes. You can filter meals by vegetarian, pescatarian, gluten-conscious, and dairy-free options. Please check each recipe's allergen information before ordering.

Where do you deliver?

We currently deliver to most of the continental US. Enter your ZIP code at checkout to confirm availability in your area.
