FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my data private?

Yes. Notes are encrypted in transit and at rest, and we never use your content to train AI models.

Can I import notes from other apps?

Quill imports from Markdown, Notion, Evernote, Apple Notes, and plain text files in a few clicks.

Does it work offline?

Yes. You can read and write offline on every device, and changes sync when you reconnect. AI features require a connection.

Can I export my notes?

Anytime. Export your entire library to Markdown or PDF. Your notes are always yours.

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Of course. Cancel in two clicks from your settings, no questions asked.
