Climate solutions shouldn't sit behind a paywall.

The tools to cut emissions and adapt to a warming world exist, but too often they're expensive, proprietary, or built without local input. We change that.

⚡

Clean energy access

Low-cost microgrid controllers and solar monitoring software that small utilities and co-ops can run themselves.

🛰️

Open climate data

Satellite and sensor pipelines that track deforestation, methane leaks, and flood risk, free for anyone to use.

🌱

Community resilience

Early-warning systems and farming advisory tools designed with, and owned by, the people who rely on them.

How we work

A simple loop that keeps our technology honest, useful, and local.

Listen

We start with community partners to define the problem before writing any code.

Build

Engineers and researchers prototype in the open with public roadmaps and reviews.

Deploy

Field teams train local operators and adapt tools to local languages and conditions.

Measure

We publish independently verified results, including what didn't work.
