About

Hi, I'm Mira.

I'm an illustrator based in a small studio full of plants, gouache stains, and half-finished sketchbooks. I trained in graphic design, but drawing always won.

My process starts with a lot of questions and a lot of scribbles. I work in a mix of traditional gouache and digital tools, building bold shapes and textured colour that stay readable at any size.
