Orange & Star Anise Short Ribs

Deeply savory with a bright citrus lift. Serve over creamy polenta or mashed potatoes.

⏱ 3 hr 15 min🍽 Serves 4🔥 Medium
Ingredients
  • 3 lb bone-in beef short ribs
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, salt & pepper
  • 1 onion, 4 garlic cloves, 2 carrots
  • 1 cup red wine, 2 cups beef stock
  • Zest and juice of 1 orange
  • 2 star anise, 2 sprigs thyme
Method
  1. Heat oven to 325°F (160°C). Season ribs generously and sear in oil until deeply browned on all sides.
  2. Remove ribs. Soften onion, carrots, and garlic in the same pot for 5 minutes.
  3. Add wine and reduce by half, scraping up the browned bits.
  4. Return ribs, add stock, orange zest and juice, star anise, and thyme.
  5. Cover and braise for 2½–3 hours until meltingly tender.
  6. Skim fat, reduce the sauce on the stovetop until glossy, and serve.
