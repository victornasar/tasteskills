The Studio

Slow architecture, carefully made.

Founded in 2008, Meridian works across housing, culture and the workplace. We begin with site: its climate, history and neighbours, and let that shape every decision that follows.

Our approach favours honest materials, passive performance and details that age well. We would rather build one thing properly than three things quickly.
