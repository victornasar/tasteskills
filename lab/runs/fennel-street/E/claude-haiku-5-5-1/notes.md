The page is built and both renders pass the checks. Here is the complete `index.html`. `DIRECTION.md` is saved alongside it in the project folder.



- **Direction:** the page is a day on a ruled line. The headline "Bread at seven, tarts by nine, gone by two" sits above a bake-day timeline, and open/closed status is the first thing on a phone.
- **Palette:** Clay and Linen from find-color (Linen ground, Rose Clay figure). Body text is 10:1; Rose Clay on Linen is 4.92:1.
- **Type:** Synonym for headlines and Sentient for body, both from find-font. Fonts are not bundled. Download them from Fontshare into `./fonts/`; until then the system fallbacks render.
- **No invented content:** no photos, reviews or prices beyond the brief. The three photo slots are labelled placeholders, and the footer Instagram link is `#` until a handle is supplied.
- **Layout:** no default hero-and-three-cards structure; the rose band is the only loud moment, reserved for cake orders.

**Open decisions (in DIRECTION.md §12):** the Instagram link, the "Message us" channel (only the phone was given), how "until we sell out" is shown, and approval of the direction. This was a non-interactive run, so the status stays draft. `tallyroll.md` was not in the project, so the "opposite of" contrast was not checked.
