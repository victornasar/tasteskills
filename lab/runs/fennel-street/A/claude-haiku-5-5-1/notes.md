Design decisions:
- Warm cream, crust-brown and fig palette with Fraunces (headings) and Figtree (body), both loaded from Google Fonts; the photo is a labelled CSS placeholder.
- The status pill computes open or closed from London time, so the phone check answers "is it open?" first.
- Menu and prices are plain lists copied verbatim; no claims, reviews or extra prices were added.
- Cake orders get a call button, and the brief gave no email, so "Message us" and the Instagram links are `#` placeholders to replace.
- Mobile-first, single column with a 16px gutter, growing to two columns for hours and address on wider screens.
