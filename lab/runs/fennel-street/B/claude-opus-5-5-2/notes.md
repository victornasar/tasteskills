- **Fonts (from find-font):** Synonym for headings and Sentient for body text, both free from Fontshare. The page loads them from `./fonts/`, so download the woff2 files into that folder before going live. Until then it falls back to system sans and Georgia. Fontshare's licence means the font files must stay out of public repos.
- **Colours (from find-color):** Club Cream (`#F5E7C3`) background with Club Burgundy (`#5B1D26`) text, 10.37:1 contrast. The skill's default was Clay and Linen, but its "Choose if" note is for sites led by food photos, and this page has only placeholders. Burgundy on cream is the skill's option for specialty shops and reads well on a phone outdoors.
- **Is it open?** The first thing on the page is an open/closed badge set from London time ("Open now · until 14:00 or sell-out" / "Closed · opens Wednesday at 7:00"), and today's row is highlighted in the hours list.
- **The tagline:** "Bread at seven, tarts by nine, gone by two" also appears as a three-point timeline: 7:00 bread, 9:00 tarts, 14:00 gone.
- **Cake orders:** one burgundy section with a tap-to-call button and a message button. On phones a bar stays at the bottom with Call and Directions. The brief gave no Instagram handle, so the Instagram links point to instagram.com until you add the bakery's profile URL.
