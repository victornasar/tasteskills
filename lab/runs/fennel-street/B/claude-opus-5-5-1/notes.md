Fonts and colours are chosen. Here is the page.



- **Fonts:** Synonym for headings and Sentient for body text. find-font ranked them top for a "warm" feel. Both come from Fontshare and are self-hosted, so put the `.woff2` files in `./fonts/` next to the page. Until you do, the page falls back to system fonts. The Fontshare licence also means keeping those files out of any public repo.
- **Colours:** I used Club Cream (burgundy `#5B1D26` on cream `#F5E7C3`, contrast 10.37:1) rather than find-color's default, Clay and Linen. That palette is meant for sites carried by food photos, and this page has none.
- **Is it open:** A sticky bar at the top shows whether the shop is open right now, in London time, and when it opens next. The hours table also marks today. This is for phone visitors who just want to check the hours.
- **Cake orders:** There's a large "48 hrs" notice with a one-tap call button. "Message us" goes to the Instagram link in the footer, which is a placeholder because the brief gives no handle; add the URL.
- **No images:** I used two labelled photo placeholders. The map is a simple street drawn in CSS with a link to OpenStreetMap, so nothing loads from an outside server.
