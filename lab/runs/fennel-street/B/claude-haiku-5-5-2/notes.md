Both skills returned results. I'm using Synonym and Sentient for the type and Clay and Linen for the colours. Synonym is the warm headline face (Switzer ranked first but is tagged neutral), and Clay and Linen is the only light direction the colour engine returned for food.



- **Type:** Synonym for the name and headings, a warm humanist sans, and Sentient for body text. Both are Fontshare (FFL) and must be self-hosted: download them into `./fonts/` as `Synonym.woff2` and `Sentient.woff2`, or the page falls back to system fonts.
- **Colour:** Clay and Linen from the colour engine, Linen ground with Rose Clay figure at 4.92:1. Body text and muted text are its derived darks at 10:1 and 4.83:1. Custard yellow isn't in this palette, so I didn't add it.
- **Open now:** the hero shows whether the shop is open, computed in London time, because customers are mostly checking that on their phones.
- **Placeholders:** photos are dashed, labelled boxes. No external images.
- **Gaps:** no Instagram URL or email was given, so Instagram is a `#` link to fill in. The phone number is a `tel:` link.
