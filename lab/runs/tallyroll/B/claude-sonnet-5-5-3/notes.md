Fonts and colours came from the find-font and find-color engines.



- **Fonts (self-hosted OFL, files go in `./fonts/`):** Steps Mono for headlines, IBM Plex Serif for body, Commit Mono for code, numbers and buttons. Steps Mono lacks `=`, `$` and `×`, so it is only used for headings. No external font requests.
- **Colours (Graphite and Signal):** graphite on near-white, with blue only for links, the open-line marker and the arithmetic result. The primary button is graphite on white at 17:1.
- **Invoice line:** the invoice line is the centrepiece, an expandable `<details>` showing 31 daily totals that sum exactly to 1,204,388. The daily values are invented, so the page labels them as illustrative placeholders. Swap in real figures if you have them.
- **Copy:** only the brief's copy is used, with no added claims or testimonials. The page is plain and left-aligned, with no imagery, and ends on pricing and the two actions.
