    How it works

    From event to invoice, in three steps.

    1. Send events.

      Post usage events from your product as they happen.

      POST /v1/events

    2. Define pricing.

      Tiers, minimums, credits and commitments, written as rules you can read.

    3. Invoice.

      Each line on an invoice opens up into the events that produced it.
