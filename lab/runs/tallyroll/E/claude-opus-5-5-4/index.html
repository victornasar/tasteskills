How it works

Three steps, from the request your product sends to the line your finance team signs off.

  1. 1

    Send events

    Post usage events from your product as they happen: POST /v1/events.

    Request

    POST /v1/events
{
  "customer": "cus_8f2a",
  "metric": "api_calls",
  "quantity": 1,
  "timestamp": "2026-03-14T09:21:07Z"
}
  2. 2

    Define pricing

    Tiers, minimums, credits and commitments, written as rules you can read.

    Rule

    metric  api_calls
price   $0.0004 per call
period  calendar month
  3. 3

    Invoice

    Each line on an invoice opens up into the events that produced it.

    Line

    API calls, March
1,204,388 calls × $0.0004 = $481.76
  ↳ 31 daily totals ↳ events
