Fonts and colours came from the engines. DIRECTION.md and index.html are saved in the project, and the full page is below.



- **Hero:** the invoice line is the hero. It is built in HTML, sits beside the headline, and opens into 31 daily totals that sum to 1,204,388. The daily numbers are my own illustration, labelled as such on the page.
- **Fonts:** Overpass for headlines, IBM Plex Sans for body, Commit Mono for code and figures, all from the font engine. I chose Overpass over the engine's top headline pick, Steps Mono, because of Steps Mono's caveats. The fonts are named with system fallbacks but not embedded, so you need to self-host the woff2 files.
- **Colour:** Graphite and Signal from the colour engine. Blue appears only on the primary button, links and focus states.
- **Cut:** no testimonials, logos, stat rows or gradients, and no claims beyond the brief. The layout checker found none of its nine overused patterns.
