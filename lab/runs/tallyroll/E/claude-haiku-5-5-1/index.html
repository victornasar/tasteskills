    01 · How it works

    Three steps from event to invoice

    1. 1 Send events. Post usage events from your product as they happen. POST /v1/events
    2. 2 Define pricing. Tiers, minimums, credits and commitments, written as rules you can read.
    3. 3 Invoice. Each line on an invoice opens up into the events that produced it.
    02 · Who it is for

    For finance

    • Line-level audit trail.
    • Exports to NetSuite and QuickBooks.
    • Revenue recognition schedules.

    For engineering

    • Idempotent event ingestion.
    • Webhooks for every invoice state.
    • SDKs for TypeScript, Python and Go.
