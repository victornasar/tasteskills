The page is saved as `index.html` and the layout check found none of the nine overused patterns. I did not re-run the check after opening the invoice line by default.



- **Structure:** The hero is left-aligned, with the example invoice line open beside it. The 31 daily totals sum to 1,204,388, and a note on the page marks them as illustrative. Then come how it works, finance and engineering side by side, pricing, a closing call to action and the footer.
- **Fonts:** Steps Mono for headings, IBM Plex Serif for body and Commit Mono for code, all open-licence and self-hosted. Put the woff2 files in `./fonts`, or the page falls back to system faces. Nothing loads from a third party. Steps Mono has only one weight and few accents, so I used it for headings only.
- **Colour:** Bottle Green Ledger, a cream ground (#F6F2E7) with deep green (#194430). Body text is 10:1 on the ground. Green marks actions and headings and nothing else.
- **Cut:** There are no stat rows, cards, testimonials or gradients. The one thing I added to the copy is a closing heading, "Check the work yourself.", and I added no claims or numbers beyond the illustrative daily totals.
